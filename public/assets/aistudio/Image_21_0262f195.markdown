## Brand & Style

The brand personality is exuberantly joyful, encouraging, delightfully tactile, and effortlessly intuitive. Designed for pet guardians, multi-pet households, and animal enthusiasts of all ages, the UI bridges communication barriers between humans and their animal companions through gamified audio translation, streak reinforcement, and pet mood logging. 

Drawing from modern gamified educational interfaces and tactile skeuomorphism, the aesthetic merges clean, spacious functional layouts with chunky, bubbly physical metaphors. UI elements present physical depth through solid bottom-edge bevels rather than blurry ambient drop shadows, mimicking tangible toy buttons and pushable tiles. High-energy micro-interactions, streak badges, bounce feedback states, and expressive character reaction modules create an emotionally rewarding, friendly environment.

## Colors

The color system communicates optimism and high-energy encouragement while preserving high readability against pure surface layers.

- **Primary Sky Blue (`#0284C7` / `#38BDF8`):** Anchors brand identity, translation states, wave forms, and primary progress arcs.
- **Secondary Sunny Amber (`#F59E0B` / `#FBBF24`):** Drives gamification drivers, XP gains, daily treat streaks, and currency badges.
- **Tertiary Playful Coral (`#F43F5E` / `#FB7185`):** Reserved for pet mood triggers, high-excitement alerts, hearts/health meters, and celebratory badges.
- **Mint Accent (`#10B981` / `#34D399`):** Indicates verified translation accuracy, successful sync, and active listening modes.
- **Neutral & Surface Tiers:** Canvas grounds on crisp `#F8FAFC`, stepping up to clean `#FFFFFF` for tactile cards. Structural borders and beveled shadow edges utilize tinted slate tones (`#CBD5E1` through `#94A3B8`) rather than muddy grays to retain color vibrancy.

## Typography

The type system is powered universally by Nunito Sans to provide extreme friendliness, high-legibility geometry, and open, soft terminal curves. Bold and heavy weights (`800` and `900`) are applied generously to titles, counters, badges, and button labels to evoke a punchy, game-native presence. Body copy retains high legibility through medium and semi-bold weights with generous line-heights, ensuring pet logs and translation transcripts read effortlessly in high-motion environments.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid architecture is optimized for single-hand mobile usage while expanding into structured card grids on larger canvases. 

- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column layout with 4-column sub-grids for quick stats. Outer canvas margin is pinned to `1rem` with a compact `1rem` column gutter. Action bars and primary translation buttons anchor to a sticky bottom thumb-zone.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters. Pet profile stats, active translation cards, and streak leaderboards reflow into a dynamic 2-column balanced dashboard.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column fixed-max constraint (1120px max width) with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer margins. Content groups into a 4-column persistent left navigation/streak sidebar and an 8-column interaction core.

Component spacing adheres strictly to an 8pt rhythmic interval (with a 4pt `space-xs` micro-step for badge padding and audio waveform bars).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects blurry atmospheric shadows in favor of a clean, comic-inspired 2.5D physical depth system:

- **The Bevel Extrusion:** Buttons, cards, and gamified progress elements feature a solid, zero-blur bottom stroke/shadow offset (Duolingo-style extrusion). Normal interactive surfaces feature a 4px solid lower edge in a darkened tint of their border or surface color (`box-shadow: 0 4px 0 0 #0369A1` on blue elements, or `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 0 #E2E8F0` on white containers).
- **Pressed & Active Dynamics:** On press or click, the element physically shifts downward by 2px to 4px via `transform: translateY(4px)`, collapsing the bottom bevel to 0px. This produces an instant, punchy mechanical response.
- **Floating Modals & Sheets:** Overlays and celebration reward dialogues feature an elevated 6px solid bottom edge paired with a 16px soft tinted ambient drop shadow (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`) against a translucent 40% slate backdrop.
- **Surface Containment:** Resting cards feature a structural 2px solid border (`#E2E8F0`) surrounding `#FFFFFF` panels to separate layers cleanly on `#F8FAFC` canvas backgrounds without visual mud.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-rounded, friendly, pill-forward visual lexicon (Level 3). 

- Standard buttons, badges, chips, and translation input fields adopt full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- Structural content cards, audio visualizer pods, and dialog boxes use expansive curved corners (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` / `1.5rem` to `2rem`).
- Avatars and reaction emoji frames are strictly circular or rounded squircles with thick uniform strokes.
- Hard 90-degree corners are entirely absent from all surface elements to preserve a safe, friendly, touchable sensation.

## Components

### Chunky 3D Push Buttons
- **Primary:** Rich `#0284C7` background, white text, 2px border `#0284C7`, solid 4px bottom shadow in `#0369A1`. Full pill radius. On click, translates down 4px with bottom shadow flattening to 0px.
- **Secondary / Streak Accent:** Cheerful `#F59E0B` surface with `#B45309` 4px bottom bevel.
- **Ghost / Neutral:** White surface, `#E2E8F0` 2px border, `#CBD5E1` 3px bottom bevel, slate-700 text.

### Micro-Badge Streaks & Counters
- Compact pill-shaped modules containing a vibrant vector icon (fire icon, bone, or paw print), bold numerical counter, and subtle saturated pastel background.
- Bordered with a 1.5px solid stroke matching the icon's darker tint. Embedded at the top of headers or floating within pet status banners.

### Pet Audio Wave Cards
- Pure white container cards with 2px solid `#E2E8F0` borders and 4px solid bottom bevels.
- Features vertical rounded pill audio visualizer bars that bounce dynamically. Inactive bars render in `#E2E8F0`; active listening bars pulse in alternating tones of Sky Blue (`#38BDF8`) and Mint (`#10B981`).
- Contains a central circular recording trigger button (56px-72px) with animated concentric pulse rings.

### Expression & Reaction Cards
- Illustrated mood badges with cartoon-style facial feedback (e.g., Happy, Hungry, Playful, Sassy).
- Highlighted selection states trigger a full card background shift to soft pastel tints (e.g., `#FEF3C7` for playful mood) with an active 3px primary border.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Pill-shaped text inputs with 2px solid `#CBD5E1` borders, scaling to `#0284C7` on focus.
- Custom radio and checkbox elements are rendered as chunky circular or squircle tokens: unchecked displays a clean 2px slate ring; checked triggers an animated bounce fill with a bold white check or paw stamp.

### Gamified Progress Trackers
- Horizontal progress bars built with full pill contours and a minimum height of 16px.
- The unfilled track is a recessed light gray (`#E2E8F0`), while the filled track showcases vibrant primary blue or amber with an internal glossy top-edge highlight bar (2px white 35% opacity) to enhance toy-like dimensionality.
